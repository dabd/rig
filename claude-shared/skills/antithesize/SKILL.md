# Antithesize

Match the operation to the user's purpose. Default to a rival thesis for an
opposing position. For falsification, robustness testing, or a broader menu of
opposition methods, read [opposition.md](references/opposition.md). A
counterexample need not be a complete worldview.

For a rival thesis, write a coherent alternative position that a reader can
understand without seeing the original. Match the genre and question of the input: an alternative
recommendation for a recommendation, or an alternative mechanism for an
explanation.

Identify the strongest defensible version of the original and its decisive
assumption. Keep established facts and actual constraints fixed. Change the
interpretation, mechanism, or value priority that produces the conclusion;
do not invent facts to sustain opposition.

The deliverable leads with the rival thesis and its positive account of how
things work. Reach a materially opposing conclusion or recommendation; a
caveat that leaves the original action unchanged is insufficient. Identify
the original's consequential failure mode, the crux separating the positions,
and evidence that could adjudicate it. Distinguish an exploratory alternative
from an endorsed factual claim. If no credible rival fits the evidence, say so.

Choose the depth and tone from the user's request. Do not require two axes,
a declared intensity setting, or a preliminary approval ceremony.
